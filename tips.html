🧭 New York Travel Tips

Smart Advice for First-Time Visitors

💡 Essential Know-Before-You-Go Tips

Navigating a massive city like New York is easy once you know how the subway, walking blocks, and local etiquette work.

📋 Quick Travel Guide

Category Tip
Subway & Transit Use contactless tap-to-pay (OMNY) with your card or phone at subway turnstiles—no paper MetroCard needed.
Best Time to Visit April–June and September–November offer mild weather and smaller crowds.
Walking & Footwear Pack comfortable walking shoes; you will easily walk 15,000+ steps a day exploring Manhattan.
Tipping Etiquette Standard tipping at sit-down restaurants and for taxi drivers is 15% to 20%.
